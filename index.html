About Me

I'm Rome, a 1st-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student at Laguna State Polytechnic University. I learn software development by building practical projects and solving real problems with code.

I'm interested in web development, Progressive Web Apps, UI/UX, and software engineering. My focus is on applications that work well on modest devices, stay usable offline when needed, and help people in practical ways.

Before I had a laptop, I worked on coding projects using a mobile device. That experience taught me resourcefulness, careful planning, and the value of shipping something useful even when the setup is not ideal.

I'm still early in my journey, and I'm serious about growing into a capable software developer who builds reliable, thoughtful tools.
