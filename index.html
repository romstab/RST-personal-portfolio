About Me

I'm a passionate 1st-year Computer Science student who learns best by building real software. I care about web development, practical problem-solving, and creating tools that actually help people.

My interests sit at the intersection of full-stack web apps, Progressive Web Apps, UI/UX, and software engineering. I focus on solutions that work offline, load quickly on modest devices, and solve concrete local needs.

I started learning and shipping projects even before I had a laptop — using a mobile phone and whatever tools were available. That constraint taught me resourcefulness, careful planning, and the value of shipping something useful rather than waiting for perfect conditions.

I'm still early in my journey, but I'm serious about growing into a capable software engineer who builds reliable, thoughtful products.
